Chapter 1

Introduction of Computer A computer is an electronic device that accepts raw data and instructions from users, processes it according to a given set of instructions, stores the processed data, and produces meaningful information as output. The word "Computer" is derived from the Latin word "Computare," which means 'to calculate'. Modern computers are versatile machines capable of performing complex mathematical calculations accurately and quickly, as well as handling tasks like creating documents, videos, games, and presentations.
